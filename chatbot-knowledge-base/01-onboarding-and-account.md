# Getting started and your account

## How do I create a Zyppi account?
<!-- kb_id: sign_up -->
To join Zyppi, open the app and tap **Continue with Google**, or sign up with your **email and a password**. After you sign up, Zyppi asks for your **mobile number**: "Add your mobile number so your rider can call you when they arrive." The number is not used to log in, and it is not checked with an OTP.

Keywords: sign up, signup, register, registration, new account, create account, mobile number

## How do I log in to Zyppi? I'm not getting an OTP.
<!-- kb_id: otp_login -->
Zyppi does **not** send an OTP to sign in, because there is no mobile-number login. Sign in with **Google**, or with your **email and password**. If you forgot your password, tap **Forgot password** on the login screen and a reset link is sent to your email. You can also tap **Continue as guest** to browse without signing in.

Keywords: login, log in, sign in, otp, otp not received, forgot password, reset password, google sign in, email login

## Can I order on Zyppi as a guest?
<!-- kb_id: guest_checkout -->
You can browse Zyppi as a guest, but guests can't order. At checkout the app says "Save your account to place an order." Link Google or an email when asked. Everything you did as a guest stays with you: your cart, addresses and favourites. After that you can sign in on any phone.

If that Google account or email already belongs to a Zyppi account, you can sign in to that account instead. Your guest orders and addresses won't move over to it. If you leave guest mode without saving, your guest orders, addresses and favourites can't be recovered.

Keywords: guest, guest mode, without login, save account, link google, link email

## How do I change the mobile number on my Zyppi account?
<!-- kb_id: phone_number -->
Zyppi uses your mobile number only so your rider can call you at delivery. You can't change it in the app. Open **Help → Chat with us** and the support team will update it for you.

Keywords: change number, update mobile, phone number, wrong number

## How do I add, change or delete a delivery address in Zyppi?
<!-- kb_id: change_address -->
Before you order, tap the **location bar on Home**. From there you can pick a saved address or add a new one: search for an area, street or landmark, label it **Home**, **Work** or **Other**, and add a landmark if it helps the rider. You can set one address as your **default** and delete old ones. Deleting an address doesn't affect past orders.

If your order is already placed and the address is wrong, tap **Talk to a person** in the support chat and the team will update the rider.

Keywords: address, change address, wrong address, add address, saved address, default address, delete address, landmark

## How do I delete my Zyppi account?
<!-- kb_id: account_delete -->
Open your profile, tap **Delete account** and type **DELETE** to confirm. This permanently deletes your profile, saved addresses, cart and favourites. Past orders are kept without your personal details, because tax records require it. You can't undo this.

- If you still have **wallet money**, the app warns you first: "Deleting your account loses your ₹… wallet balance for good."
- You **can't** delete your account while an order, payment or reported problem is still in progress. Wait until it's finished.

Keywords: delete account, close account, remove data, deactivate, privacy, account deletion
